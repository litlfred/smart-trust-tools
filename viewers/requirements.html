STT-SEL-004 · One or more payloads per HCERT

SHALL functional derived kotlin rust open in explorer · node

The issuer SHALL accept one or more payloads and emit an hcert (-260) map with one entry per payload, refusing a request with no payload (NoPayload) or two payloads under the same subclaim (DuplicateSubclaim). The verifier SHALL accept an hcert map with any number of entries and return each valid entry with its subclaim, in wire order. It SHALL fail with HcertMissing when there is none, and with MultipleSubclaims when there are more entries than the caller's optional maxPayloads allows. An entry whose payload fails its model check SHALL be left out of the result and reported as a diagnostic; verification SHALL fail with that entry's own reason only when no valid entry remains.
