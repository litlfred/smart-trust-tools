Enumerate the source descriptor candidate, extracted

Resolve the request against the source's descriptor (one per corpus, in the declared sources/ of the layer that owns it, e.g. who-iris/sources/), read against cat-harness/schemas/library/source-descriptor.ts BEFORE any gate. Three things come from it and none may be guessed. (1) Which subset strategies the source supports — supportsSubset(). (2) Whether a subset stands on its own: when subsetIsSelfContained is false (mathlib), the request is closed over its dependencies here, and every gate after this one is asked about the CLOSURE, because a subset that omits what its members depend on is not a smaller corpus, it is one that does not build. A source with no descriptor is not an error here: it is an unknown, and SIZE refuses on it. The descriptor is CALLED here rather than restated.
