Note
Read at commit 883291d51e206719c1417cf95393a5cd8b20dc4b (2026-07-21). Three GITB test suites: Track 1 HCERT VHL, QR to verified LAC IPS bundle; Track 2 HCERT ICVP, QR to ICVP-IPS conformance; Track 3 HCERT PH4H, QR to MeOW bundle. Each decodes an uploaded QR image through the gdhcn-helper service (decode/hcert, verify/signature against the GDHCN DEV trust list, extract/reference, shlink/authorize, shlink/fetch-fhir), then validates or transforms the FHIR content with Matchbox. Test data: test-data/Track1_VHL_1234_2026.jpg, Track2_ICVP_2026.jpg, Track3_MeOW_2026.jpg. The repository carries no licence file, so its test data is cited here, not copied.
